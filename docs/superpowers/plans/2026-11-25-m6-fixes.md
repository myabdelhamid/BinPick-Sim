## Causes (M5 run and dev seeds 1500–1519)
1. Path failures before the lift (5 of 24 failures): the lift ends above the arm's reach at the far bin edge. In set-down: the wrist was solved 2° from joint 7's limit.
2. Drops in transport (2, both bins lost): 1.7 kg at 2 rad/s² uses 100% of a curved grip, 91% of a flat one; at 1.2–1.5 it uses 83–91%.
3. Cycle 8.09 s: all parts carried at 3 rad/s² whatever their weight; stops between moves; retreat and transport heights larger than needed. Planning is charged 0.2 s but takes about 1.0 s: speed it up and charge what it takes.
