# Milestone 2 (Motion + State Machine + Seal Model v1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (owner chose native execution). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** The arm picks a part from a known pose in the settled pile and places it in a tray slot. A full capture → plan → approach → grip → lift → transport → place → verify state machine drives it. Suction holds or fails according to a physical seal model, and every attempt is logged with an outcome and failure reason.

**Architecture:**
- `motion/ik.py`: IK on an arm-only model (fast), with any approach direction.
- `motion/trajectory.py`: time-parameterized joint trajectories, with straight-line Cartesian segments where the cup moves along its axis.
- `sim/suction.py`: seal model from ground-truth lip rays, which sets the adhesion force and drives a noisy vacuum sensor.
- `planning/oracle.py`: picks a grasp from a part's known pose. This is a stand-in until M3 perception.
- `supervisor/`: state machine, placement maths, tray-camera verify, ground-truth referee, attempt records.
- `cli.py`: demo command with CSV and MP4 output.

**Tech Stack:** as M1, plus `imageio-ffmpeg` for MP4.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-binpick-sim-design.md` (§3 metrics, §7 suction realism and sensing, §8 placement rules)

## Design decisions for M2 (builder's calls within spec §7)

**Seal model v1** (`sim/suction.py`, evaluated every control step while suction is on):
- Take 16 points on the cup lip circle (r = 22 mm) in the cup-face plane. Cast each one along the cup axis against the **gripped/target part's geom only** (`mju_rayGeom`), starting 30 mm behind the face.
- If any lip ray misses the part, the lip is over an edge or the face is too small, and `q = 0`.
- Otherwise `spread = max(d) − min(d)` over the lip hit distances. Tilt, curvature and edge rounding all show up as spread.
- `q = 1` for `spread ≤ gap_ok` (3 mm). It falls linearly to `q = 0` at `spread = gap_max` (8 mm, the bellows compliance).
- Contact gating: `q = 0` if `min(d) > contact_dist` (6 mm), meaning the cup isn't touching.
- Adhesion actuator ctrl = `q` (gain = 3 kg × g = 29.4 N). Detachment comes from MuJoCo physics: weight, inertia and the peel torque from an off-center center of mass. It is never scripted.
- Cup geom: `margin = gap = 4 mm`, so adhesion sees the surface just before touching without pushing on it.
- **Vacuum sensor:** `p = −vacuum_kpa · q + N(0, noise)`. Sealed when `p < −sealed_threshold_kpa` (−40 kPa). The supervisor reads only this, never `q`.
- Sanity cases these must produce (tests):
  - flat top, centered → holds a 2.4 kg steel part through lift and transport;
  - a 30° face with the cup vertical → `q = 0`;
  - side of a 40 mm cylinder → `q = 0`;
  - cup half over a box edge → `q = 0`.

**Motion:**
- Joint moves use quintic time scaling. Duration = max over joints of the vel/acc-limited time (`max_joint_vel` 1.5 rad/s, `max_joint_acc` 4 rad/s²).
- Approach, lift and place descents are straight lines along the cup axis. IK runs every 10 mm, then the path is time-scaled with `max_cart_vel` 0.4 m/s and `max_cart_acc` 1.5 m/s². The acceleration limit keeps 2.5 kg × (g + a) under the 29.4 N rated hold.
- The Panda's own position actuators track `ctrl = q_des(t)`.

**Cycle:**
1. capture (`capture_s` 0.3 s of sim time, for camera and compute)
2. plan (`plan_s` 0.2)
3. move to pre-grasp: 120 mm back along the face normal
4. approach along −normal to 2 mm past the surface
5. suction on, wait `vacuum_build_s` 0.15, read the sensor; not sealed → `no_seal`
6. lift along +normal by 60 mm, then straight up to rim + part height + 50 mm
7. joint move to pre-place, with the wrist yawed so the part's long side lines up with tray x
8. descend until the part's lowest point is 5 mm above the tray
9. suction off, wait `release_s` 0.25
10. retreat 100 mm up
11. verify (`verify_s` 0.2)

The sensor is polled during lift and transport; a loss gives `seal_lost_lift` or `seal_lost_transport`.

**Known-pose oracle (M2 only):**
- For a chosen part, find the face whose outward normal is closest to +z, with tilt ≤ 20°. That is a box face, or a cylinder end or side.
- The grasp point is the face center, nudged toward the part's projected center of mass if the cup lip would fall off the face.
- Reject the part if a vertical ray from above any lip point hits another body first (occluded).
- Part height for placement comes from the known dims. This is flagged for M3, where it has to come from perception.

**Verify (real sensors only):** the tray camera's depth inside the slot rectangle gives the pixels more than 3 mm above the tray surface. Back-project their centroid. `place_ok` if it is within 10 mm of the slot center and the vacuum sensor reads released.

**Referee (ground truth, scoring only):** the part's center xy is within ±10 mm of the slot center (x and y), and the part sits on the tray (its z is below the tray surface + its max dim).

**Lost parts:** a part that ends outside the bin and not in a slot is parked off-cell and recorded as lost (spec §3).

## Global Constraints

- All M1 constraints hold. New tunables go in `configs/default.yaml` under `suction`, `motion` and `timing`.
- Supervisor code must not read part poses from `scene.data`, except through `planning/oracle.py` (M2's declared known-pose input) and the referee.
- Development seeds ≥ 1000 only.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. Off-center grasps on long, heavy parts: they must peel or slide off under physics, not stay glued. (Test in Task 3.)
2. A cup touching a *neighbouring* part as well as the target: the seal still uses only the target's geometry, and adhesion only pulls whatever the cup touches. The supervisor must notice two parts lifted. (Noted; full handling in M4.)
3. The trajectory passing through the bin wall or the pile during transport. Lift height must clear the rim plus the part's height. (Test in Task 6.)
4. A slot already occupied: placement uses the next free slot; the counter is never reused. (Test in Task 6.)
5. The tracking error of the position-controlled arm under a 2.5 kg load causing the place to miss ±10 mm. (Test in Task 6.)

### Task 1: Arm-only IK with approach direction
**Files:** modify `src/binpick/motion/ik.py`, `src/binpick/sim/scene.py` (expose `build_arm_spec`); test `tests/test_reach.py`
**Interfaces (produces):**
- `solve_cup_pose(scene, target_pos, approach_dir, yaw=0.0, q0=None, ...) -> IKResult`. The cup axis points along `approach_dir` (unit vector, cup moving toward the part). `yaw` is about that axis. The yaw + π fallback is kept.
- `solve_cup_down(...)` = `solve_cup_pose(..., approach_dir=(0,0,-1))`
- `ArmIK` cached per Scene (`scene.ik`), built from an arm-only compiled model with the same base pose. Tests: existing reach tests stay green; a new test checks a 20°-tilted approach is reached with axis error < 0.01 rad; a speed test requires mean solve time < 25 ms.

### Task 2: Trajectories and tracking
**Files:** create `src/binpick/motion/trajectory.py`; test `tests/test_trajectory.py`
**Interfaces:**
- `JointTrajectory(t: np.ndarray, q: np.ndarray)` with `sample(t) -> q` and `duration`
- `joint_move(q0, q1, mcfg) -> JointTrajectory` (quintic)
- `cartesian_line(scene, q0, p0, p1, approach_dir, yaw, mcfg) -> JointTrajectory` (raises `IKError` if any waypoint fails)
- `execute(scene, traj, on_step=None) -> bool`. Steps the sim, sets arm ctrl, and calls `on_step(scene)` every control step; returns False if `on_step` returns False (abort).

Tests: vel/acc limits respected; endpoints exact; tracking (cup tip vs planned) < 3 mm on a park→bin→tray loop.

### Task 3: Seal model, vacuum sensor, suction
**Files:** create `src/binpick/sim/suction.py`; test `tests/test_suction.py`
**Interfaces:**
- `seal_quality(scene, part_index) -> float`
- `SuctionCup(scene, rng)` with `on(part_index)`, `off()`, `update()`, `pressure_kpa() -> float` and `sealed() -> bool`
- `place_part(scene, i, pos, quat)` test helper, kept in `tests/helpers.py`

Tests: the four sanity cases above; lifting a 2.4 kg steel box centered holds; an off-center grasp on a long steel bar (the cup at one end) detaches during lift; the sensor reads ≈ −60 kPa sealed and ≈ 0 off.
